# SOLUTIONS

## S1 (4 marks)

(a) "…a variant of **stochastic beam search** in which successor states are generated by **combining two parent states rather than by modifying a single state**." *(1 each blank)*

(b) **Start** (random population) → **Fitness** (evaluate each) → **New population** (selection, crossover, mutation, accepting until full) → **Replace** → **Test** (termination) → **Loop**. *(2; half marks lost for listing the three operators as "the steps")*

## S2 (6 marks)

Positions (col, row): (1,5) (2,2) (3,4) (4,6) (5,3) (6,3) (7,1) (8,7)

```
8 | . . . . . . . .
7 | . . . . . . . Q
6 | . . . Q . . . .
5 | Q . . . . . . .
4 | . . Q . . . . .
3 | . . . . Q Q . .
2 | . Q . . . . . .
1 | . . . . . . Q .
    ---------------
    1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
```

Left-to-right scan, only looking rightward. Rows used: 5, 2, 4, 6, 3, 3, 1, 7 — the repeat is row 3:

- **col 5 & col 6** — both row 3 → **same row** *(1)*
- **col 5 & col 7** — rows 3 → 1: Δc = 2, Δr = 2 → **diagonal** *(2)*
- All other pairs: Δc ≠ Δr and rows differ. Notable near-misses to check and reject: col 1–col 3 (Δ2/Δ1), col 2–col 5 (Δ3/Δ1), col 4–col 8 (Δ4/Δ1). *(1 for showing the scan)*

Same-row = 1, diagonal = 1, **attacks = 2**

**fitness = C(8,2) − 2 = 28 − 2 = 26** *(2: one for 28, one for the subtraction)*

> Mark-loser: answering "2". Fitness is the **non-attacking** count, 26.

## S3 (6 marks)

Evaluate — show the arithmetic:

- `1111` → x = 15 → 225 − 75 = **150**
- `0100` → x = 4 → 16 − 20 = **−4 → clamped to 0**
- `1010` → x = 10 → 100 − 50 = **50**
- `0111` → x = 7 → 49 − 35 = **14**

Total = 150 + 0 + 50 + 14 = **214** *(2, incl. the clamp)*

| # | Chromosome | x | f(x) | p = f/214 | Cumulative bin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1111` | 15 | 150 | 0.701 | 0.001 – 0.701 |
| 2 | `0100` | 4 | 0 *(raw −4)* | 0 | — none — |
| 3 | `1010` | 10 | 50 | 0.234 | 0.702 – 0.935 |
| 4 | `0111` | 7 | 14 | 0.065 | 0.936 – 1.000 |
| | **Total** | | **214** | **1.000** | |

Check: 0.701 + 0.234 + 0.065 = 1.000 ✓ *(2 for the table)*

Draws: **0.43** → 0.001–0.701 → **`1111`** · **0.87** → 0.702–0.935 → **`1010`** · **0.95** → 0.936–1.000 → **`0111`** *(2)*

> Mark-loser: giving the clamped `0100` a bin. It gets none — string 3's bin starts at 0.702, directly after string 1's. Insert a bin for it and both later draws land on the wrong parent.
